Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Summary: Billsheet collects no data. There is no bank connection, no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your bills, subscriptions, contracts and payments stay on your iPhone, reminders are local notifications, and files you export go only where you send them.

1. Who we are

Billsheet is an iPhone app provided by Sergei Stasenko (“we”).

2. Data we do not collect

We don't collect, receive or store any personal data through the app. Billsheet has no sign-in and no account, never connects to your bank, we run no server for it, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs. The app doesn't track you across apps or websites, doesn't use the advertising identifier and never asks for tracking permission.

3. Your bills and payments

Everything you enter (items, amounts, due dates, contract dates, cancellation notes, payment links, notes and sheets) and every payment you mark is stored on your iPhone only, in the app's own storage. A few settings, such as your reminder days and time and your currency, are kept in the app's preferences on the device. We can't see, read or recover any of it.

Like other app data, your sheet is included in your iPhone backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to your computer. Those backups belong to your Apple Account or your computer, and we have no access to them. Billsheet doesn't sync your data between devices.

4. Reminders and background refresh

Reminders before a due date and before a cancel-by date are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent through a server. The app asks for permission the first time you save an item with a reminder, and you can turn reminders off in More › Reminders or in the iPhone's Settings. Mark paid in a reminder records the payment on your iPhone. To keep the reminders and autopay marks up to date, iOS lets Billsheet run briefly in the background now and then; it works only with the data on your iPhone and sends nothing.

5. Widgets

The widgets read what is still due and the next payments from a file the app keeps in a shared folder on your iPhone; that file never leaves the device.

6. Backups and exports

The backups Billsheet makes are files on your iPhone. When you share a backup file or export a CSV file, the file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you send it with the share sheet (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop or another app). When you import a file, it is read on your iPhone. A payment link you saved opens in your browser only when you tap it. These transfers are handled by Apple or by the app you choose, not by us.

7. Purchases

The optional Lifetime Unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether the unlock has been bought on your Apple Account. Apple's handling of this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Ratings

Once, after you have marked your third payment paid, the app may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Whether and how you rate is handled entirely by Apple; the app only keeps, on your iPhone, how many payments you have marked and whether it has asked.

9. Emails to support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send, including the app version, iOS version and device that the in-app support email adds. We use this only to answer you, don't share it, and delete it when it is no longer needed for your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete single items and payments, everything with More › Delete All Data, and backup versions in More › Backups. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request, apart from emails you have sent us.

11. Children

Billsheet is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you and to complain to a data protection authority. Since the app collects no data, these rights concern only emails you have sent us; write to ssx51f@gmail.com.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts to collect data, the policy will say so before that version is released.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, write to ssx51f@gmail.com.

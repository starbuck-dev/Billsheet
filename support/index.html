Frequently asked questions

How do I add a bill, a subscription or a contract?

On the Month tab, tap Add a bill (or +). Type the amount, or switch on Variable if it changes every time (the last amount is then the estimate), choose how it repeats — “Monthly · on the 15th” means the 15th, not every 30 days — give it a name and tap Save item. For a gym, phone or insurance contract, switch on This is a contract and fill in the start, the minimum term, the notice period and what it renews for: the editor shows the date you have to cancel by and when you will be reminded.

What is free, and what doesn't count?

Free, for good: up to six active items of any kind — bills, subscriptions and contracts — with all payment history, contracts with their cancel-by dates, reminders, widgets, import, export and backups. Ended items (a cancelled contract, a bill past its end date) and archived items don't count, and the Month tab shows how many free items you have used before you add one. The one-time Lifetime Unlock adds unlimited items and several sheets (personal, family, business). Nothing you entered or exported is ever locked, and an import always comes in whole. There is no subscription and no ads; the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

How do I import my bills from the app I used before?

Export your bills as a CSV file from the app or the spreadsheet you used before. In Billsheet, tap More › Import › Import a CSV file and choose the file. Billsheet detects the columns (name, amount, due date and how often it repeats) and the date format, lets you assign a column it could not recognise, shows a preview with the number of items, and imports every row — also on the free tier: if the file brings more than six items, all of them come in, and you can archive some of them or unlock. Import the sample file shows what a file that works looks like.

Why doesn't Billsheet connect to my bank or cancel contracts for me?

Because your sheet should stay yours. Billsheet never asks for a bank login and never sends anything anywhere: you add each bill once, and it repeats by itself. Billsheet doesn't pay bills and doesn't send cancellations either — it tells you the last day you can still cancel, reminds you 14 and 3 days before, and keeps a note of when you cancelled. You cancel with the provider, the way your contract says; then tap I've cancelled this contract on the item, and it moves to Ended.

Why didn't a reminder arrive?

Reminders are local notifications from your iPhone. Check that notifications for Billsheet are allowed in the iPhone's Settings › Notifications (More › Reminders shows Open Settings when they are off) and that a Focus does not silence them. More › Reminders sets how many days before the due date you are reminded and at what time; contracts are reminded 14 and 3 days before the cancel-by date. A payment marked paid is not reminded again, and an autopay item marks itself paid on the due day without a reminder. Billsheet plans the reminders again every time you open it and in the background; if Background App Refresh is off for Billsheet, open the app now and then so the next reminders are planned.

How do I mark a payment as paid?

Swipe the row on the Month tab and tap Mark paid, use Mark paid in the reminder itself without opening the app, or mark it in the item's payment history. For a variable bill you can type the actual amount. Items with Autopay on mark themselves paid on the due day, tagged “auto”, with Undo if the money did not go out.

What do the groups, the Calendar and the widgets show?

Set your payday in More › Payday (a day of the month, the last working day or every two weeks), and the Month tab groups the payments before payday, after payday and paid; without a payday it groups them by week. The Calendar tab shows the month as a grid with the amounts under the dates and marks payday and cancel-by days. The widgets show what is still due this month, the next three payments, and the next payment on the Lock Screen.

Where are my backups?

Billsheet makes a backup on your iPhone once a day after you add, change or mark something, and before an import, a restore or Delete All Data; the last ten automatic versions are kept. Tap Back up now in More › Backups for a version of your own, which is never removed automatically. Share on a version saves it as a file to Files, a computer or another device.

How do I restore a backup?

Choose a version in More › Backups and tap Restore, or tap Restore from file… to open a backup file. You first see a preview of what will be added; Merge into my data then adds it. Restoring always merges: nothing is deleted or overwritten, and your current data is backed up automatically first.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought the unlock with, then tap Restore Purchases in More › Purchase or on the unlock screen. The purchase belongs to your Apple Account, so it works on every iPhone you use with it.

How do I export or delete my data?

More › Export gives Export items as CSV and Export payments as CSV: every item and every payment, for Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets. To remove everything, use More › Delete All Data: every sheet, item and payment goes after a last backup, and the backups stay until you delete them in More › Backups. Deleting the app removes all of its data, backups included, from your iPhone. We hold no copy, because nothing is ever sent to us.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Billsheet runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later, in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Russian. Amounts, dates and the first day of the week follow your iPhone's region. It works fully offline.
